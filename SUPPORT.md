# Getting help

- **Questions, ideas and show-and-tell:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/jmbost20/threadglyph/discussions). Search first; someone may have asked already.
- **Bugs:** [open a bug report](https://github.com/jmbost20/threadglyph/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) with your Obsidian and Threadglyph versions and the steps that show it. Use made-up text, never your real notes.
- **Feature requests:** check the [roadmap](docs/roadmap.md), then [open a feature request](https://github.com/jmbost20/threadglyph/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **Security problems:** report them privately, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) describes. Never in a public issue.
- **Conduct concerns:** see the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

Threadglyph is maintained by one person on a best-effort basis. Expect a reply within about a week.
